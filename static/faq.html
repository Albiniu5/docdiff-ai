Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is AI document comparison?

AI document comparison is highly accurate because it understands the context and semantic meaning of text, not just raw character differences. Unlike traditional "diff" tools, it can detect moved paragraphs, rephrased sentences, and conceptual changes, making it superior for legal contracts, technical manuals, and complex agreements.

Can I compare PDFs and Word files?

Yes, Compare Docs AI supports cross-format comparison. You can upload a PDF and compare it directly against a Word document (DOCX) or a plain text file. Our system extracts text from both formats to handle the comparison seamlessly.

Is document comparison secure?

Security is our top priority. We use in-memory processing, meaning your files are analyzed in RAM and never saved to a permanent database or disk storage. Once the comparison session is complete, the data is automatically wiped from our server memory.

What types of changes are detected?

Our AI detects three main categories of changes: Additions (new content), Deletions (removed content), and Modifications (rewritten or changed text). It can also highlight subtle changes in meaning that standard tools might miss.

Can I compare scanned documents?

Currently, we support text-based PDFs. If your PDF is a scanned image, you may need to use OCR (Optical Character Recognition) software to convert it to selectable text before uploading. We are working on integrating OCR features in future updates.
